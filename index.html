Attendance Management (Admin Override)

Override any staff member's daily attendance. Deductions apply instantly to salary: Absent = 100% of one day's basic salary, Half Day = 50%. Present, Approved Leave, Holiday and Sundays have no deduction. Choose "— No Override (Auto) —" to remove an override. Automatic login marking never overwrites these overrides, and holidays or non-working days are never marked Absent automatically.
